# Code of conduct

Proavus is about families – often about people who are no longer here, and
sometimes about painful stories: illegitimate children, emigration, war,
persecution. Treat the people you talk to here, and the people in the
records, with the same respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not programmers, and old
  handwriting is hard for everyone.
- Disagree about the evidence, not about the person. "This record does not
  prove it" is fine; mocking someone's reading or their family is not.
- Respect privacy. Never post data about real families, above all living
  people, and never ask others to do so ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Accept a "no" to a feature with grace; the reasons will be given.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, sexualised language or images.
- Attacks based on origin, nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender,
  sexual orientation, disability or age – including about ancestors.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent
  (doxxing), including findings about someone else's family.
- Using genealogy to support claims of racial or ethnic superiority.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions and, for
serious or repeated breaches, block a person from the project. Report a
problem privately to the maintainer through a
[private report](SECURITY.md#how-to-report) – mark it as a conduct report;
it will not be made public.

This code applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.
